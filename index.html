Player Profile

Jeferson Romero is a rising young soccer prospect competing with Solar SC U15 Academy. A right-footed Center Back and Holding Midfielder, Jeferson brings defensive versatility, leadership, and competitive experience to the field.

He has represented Honduras at the U-15 national-team level, receiving three national-team call-ups. Jeferson has also competed in ECNL, Copa Oro, and the Texas Champions Cup.
